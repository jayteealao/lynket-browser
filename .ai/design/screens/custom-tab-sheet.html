Custom Tab as a sheet

With "Open as a sheet" on in Slide over, a link opens over the app the person came from. The browser draws the sheet and its toolbar; this screen shows the result so the behaviour can be judged. Page text and sizes are illustrative. The devices are drawn in the light theme only; the browser draws the sheet in its own theme.

Phone: bottom sheet at half height

The browser draws the sheet at half the screen (D-003). The chat stays in view above it but takes no taps (D-014); a quick drag down, the close button or Back closes the sheet.
