Native Bubbles (Beta)

Native bubbles are in beta. Android shows them only when Lynket's bubble setting is "All conversations can bubble".

Known issues

  • Pages in a bubble open in Lynket's web view, not in your browser.
  • Android shows five bubbles at a time; more go to its overflow.
  • Some menu items are hidden in a bubble.
  • When Android does not show a bubble, the page opens in a tab.
