Paragraph | The harbour lights in the old town were switched on again on Saturday evening, and they will stay on from dusk to dawn until the end of March, the harbour office said.
Paragraph | The lights mark the entrance channel for small boats, which come in late in the season when the fishing fleet returns from the northern grounds before the storms.
Paragraph | Residents can see the full list of lights and their hours on the notice board next to the harbour office, or ask the harbour master in person.
